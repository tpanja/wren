# Fonos app shell (Sing, Progress, Band)

Mode: Operate. Native iOS, SwiftUI. Code-led build. Specified in Docs/UI-REDESIGN.md; rebuild pending spec approval.

Audience and job: singers practicing alone with the throat band, beginners by default, voice students via the Technical detail setting. Jobs: sing a take and know the one thing to fix (Sing), see improvement over weeks (Progress), keep the band fitted (Band).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The coach that feels the voice. A recovery-tracker shell (WHOOP rings, Apple Health cards) where every number comes from sound plus throat vibration, and every take ends in one focus and a drill. Refuses the default pitch-app look of a neon needle on black and a wall of scores.

OWN-WORLD: Dark slate ground #0F1316, cards #1A2025, warm off-white ink. Stream colors only for sensors: mic amber #F2B33D, left accel teal #3CC8B4, right accel violet #8E7CF0. Ring colors: In tune sky #5AA9E6, Steady rose #E77FA3, Range lime #A6CE5C. State tokens separate from metric hues (recording coral, caution amber, trends in ink with arrows). Type: Sora for numerals, titles, and tracked caps; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading text (user requested non-default fonts; Atkinson chosen for legibility). Custom 24 pt / 1.8 pt-stroke icon set, no SF Symbols (user request).

STORY: Open Fonos, see today's practice and the suggested exercise, tap Sing, watch your pitch line land on the notes, read one focus with a drill, sing again. Progress shows rings and range growing against your own baseline.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sing tab. Toolbar: settings icon, status pill, battery. Today card with three mini rings. Live throat strip (left/right fold, 72 pt). Up-next exercise card with the 132 pt Sing button centered below.

FORM: Extension of the established prototype world (pinned by the user's WHOOP and Apple Health screenshots). Signature move: the pitch lane, the singer's pitch line laid over target-note bars, with the throat fold beneath it. seed: none (pinned by brief)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
